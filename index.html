About Us

Built by Hand.
Backed by Experience.

Clear Creek Builders was founded in 2014 by Alan Williams right here in Strafford, New Hampshire. What started as a focused residential construction operation has grown into one of central NH's trusted names for new homes, additions, and full site development.

Alan and his crew take every project personally. When you work with Clear Creek, you're working directly with the people swinging the hammers — not a salesman who hands you off to a crew you've never met.

Clear Creek Builders is fully licensed and insured in the State of New Hampshire. We pull every permit, pass every inspection, and don't cut corners — because your home is too important for anything less.
